Our Story

Built on Genuine Hospitality

Palace City Hotel opened its doors over fifteen years ago with a simple belief: that true luxury lies in comfort, warmth and attention to detail. What began as a modest boutique property has since grown into one of the city's most trusted names in hospitality, welcoming business travellers, families and celebrations alike.

Every room, every dish and every interaction is guided by the same standard our founders set from day one — treat every guest like family.
